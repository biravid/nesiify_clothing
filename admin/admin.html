How to Add to Catalogue

  1. Place your product images into assets/products/ (e.g. NC009-1.jpg).
  2. Fill out the form and click GENERATE PRODUCT.
  3. Click [ COPY JSON ] to copy the object.
  4. Open data/products.json in your editor and paste the object into the array.
  5. Save the file — your new product will immediately appear on the shop page!

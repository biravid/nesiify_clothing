Returns & Exchange Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Nesiify Clothing

1. Size Exchange Policy for Kids

Children grow rapidly and fit comfort is essential. If an item you ordered does not fit your child comfortably, we offer a hassle-free size exchange within 7 days from the date of delivery.

To request a size exchange, simply open your WhatsApp chat with us, share your order code, and let us know what replacement size you need.

2. Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for an exchange, the product must meet the following criteria:

  • Unused, unworn, and unwashed
  • In original packaging with all brand tags intact
  • Free of stains, scents, or signs of wear
  • Requested within 7 calendar days of receipt

3. How to Initiate an Exchange

1. Message us directly on WhatsApp with your Product Code and delivery details.
2. Let us know the new size required for your child.
3. Our team will verify inventory availability and guide you through the return pickup or courier arrangement.
4. Once the returned piece is inspected, your exchange parcel is dispatched.

4. Damaged or Defective Items

Every Nesiify Clothing parcel is packed with genuine care. In the rare event that an item arrives damaged or with a manufacturing defect, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours with a quick photo of the defect, and we will promptly send a replacement at zero extra cost.

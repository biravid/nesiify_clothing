Logistics & Delivery

Shipping Policy

Last Updated: January 2026 • Nesiify Clothing

1. Order Processing Time

All kidswear orders are confirmed manually via WhatsApp chat. Once order details (sizes, colours, and delivery address) and payment are verified, packages are carefully packed and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours.

2. Delivery Timeframes & Coverage

We ship pan-India through trusted courier partners. Typical transit timelines are as follows:

  • Metro Cities: 3 to 5 business days from dispatch
  • Rest of India: 4 to 7 business days from dispatch
  • Remote / Northeast Locations: 6 to 9 business days

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable based on the order size and delivery pincode, are clearly calculated and confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp before payment. There are no hidden surprises or unexpected checkout fees.

4. Real-Time Tracking via WhatsApp

Once your parcel has been handed over to the courier partner, our team will send your tracking AWB link directly to your WhatsApp chat so you can follow the transit status in real time.

5. Delay or Transit Issues

If your package is delayed or marked delivered without you receiving it, message us immediately on WhatsApp or email contact@nesiifyclothing.com with your order details and we will coordinate with the courier logistics team.

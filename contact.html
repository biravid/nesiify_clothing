Frequently Asked Questions

What age range does Nesiify Clothing cater to?

Our kidswear collection is designed specifically for children aged 2 to 12 years, focusing on daily wear with comfort and genuine quality.

How do I place an order?

Simply select your desired item, choose your child's size and preferred colour, and tap "ORDER ON WHATSAPP". Our team will confirm stock, provide direct payment details (UPI/GPay/PhonePe), and coordinate dispatch.

What if the size doesn't fit my child?

We offer easy 7-day size exchanges. Just message us on WhatsApp with your product code and we will help exchange it for the appropriate size.
